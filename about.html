About Lux Eterna

We make cinematic work for brands and storytellers — grounded in craft, texture, and editorial patience.

Our approach

Lux Eterna blends a film-first discipline with commercial sensibilities. We lead productions with a director-plus-DP mindset, shaping image, motion and sound to serve story and emotion.
